ABOUT THE COMPANY

EVERY STAR
HAS A STORY.

新しい星が、今ここから輝き出す。

Founded in Tokyo, Japan in 2026 by creator and manager Lex Luaris, STARDUST ENTERTAINMENT began with a single dream and its very first spark: solo idol RIYA ☆ (Riyana Nazukii). Our Tokyo headquarters houses specialized vocal, dance, and A&R production departments dedicated to developing dynamic J-Pop idols—from crafting original albums and music videos to staging unforgettable live concerts.

Our Tokyo headquarters houses specialized training departments dedicated to developing multi-faceted J-Pop artists across music.
