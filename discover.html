PERSONA INTRODUCTION

LEX LUARIS

レックス・ルアリス · マネージャー

An adult Graphic Designer and Manager in Tokyo for Stardust Entertainment shy when meeting people for the first time, yet kind, straightforward, and full of warmth once she feels comfortable.

Around people she is close to, Lex becomes talkative and even loud whenever she talks about her favorite interests and things she loves. She enjoys skating on rollerblades, exploring new places, taking in scenery, and speaking her mind whenever she wants.

She is always happy spending time with someone she loves, and she has zero patience for untrustworthy people who break trust something that makes her genuinely mad.
